Tourist Sentiment Analysis

Turning traveler opinions into meaningful insights.

Sentiment analysis is an NLP technique that reads free-form text and decides whether the writer feels positive, negative or neutral. Here, a pretrained multilingual transformer reads a tourist review written in any of 16 supported languages — English, Spanish, French, German, Hindi, Japanese, Chinese, Arabic and more — and labels it automatically.

Analyze Review About the project
Three outcomes

Every review lands in one of three classes

The classifier is trained on labelled reviews and predicts one of these sentiments.

Positive

The visitor enjoyed the place: praise for views, service, food or value.

“Breathtaking sunset and very friendly guides.”

Negative

The visitor was unhappy: complaints about cleanliness, crowding, cost or behaviour.

“Dirty, overcrowded and the staff were rude.”

Neutral

Mostly factual or balanced text without a strong emotional lean.

“The fort opens at 9 am and has a small museum.”

How it works

From raw opinion to a clear label

1

You submit a review

Type or paste a tourist review in any supported language on the Analyze page. It is sent to the Flask backend.

2

The model reads it

The language is detected (or chosen by you), the text is tokenized into subwords and passed to the multilingual transformer. Nothing is translated.

3

You get the insight

The predicted sentiment, confidence and language are shown, and saved so the Dashboard can summarise real usage.
